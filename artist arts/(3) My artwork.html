1. Radhe krishna.

Artist :- Sneha Patel

✧ Description ✧
This beautiful devotional artwork depicts the eternal and spiritual bond between the Hindu deities Lord Krishna and Radha. The composition shows Radha gently resting her head against Krishna’s face, perfectly capturing a serene aura of peace, devotion, and profound affection. The artist has put immense effort into the intricate details of the clothing textures, the elaborate jewelry pieces, and the traditional ornaments adorning Krishna’s peacock-feathered crown. 10. Child

Artist :- Sneha Patel

✧ Description ✧
This hand-drawn pencil artwork is titled "Child with Water Droplets" (often created as a specialized study in hyper-realistic textures and liquid rendering). The portrait beautifully showcases a young child looking forward with an innocent, intense, and calm facial expression rather than a sad one. The defining feature of this piece is the masterfully rendered water droplets and wet sheen cascading down the child's face, hair, and shoulders. The artist has achieved a powerful three-dimensional effect through expert pencil shading and high-contrast charcoal work. By contrasting deep, dark shadows against bright, reflective highlights, the drawing perfectly mimics the physics of light reflecting off individual drops of clear water sitting on skin. It stands out as a highly technical demonstration of realism in pencil art.

16. Water With Ice

Artist :- Sneha Patel

✧ Description ✧
This sketch is a brilliant textbook example of still life drawing, focusing on a clear drinking glass filled to the brim with large ice cubes. The true challenge and success of this piece lie in how the artist captured the complex physics of light, reflection, and transparency. By carefully mapping out sharp highlights alongside dark, structural shadows, the sketch perfectly replicates the look of solid, freezing ice and reflective glass contours. The masterful execution of cross-hatching and smooth pencil blending gives the entire object an incredibly convincing, tangible, and photorealistic quality.

17. Shapes

✧ Description ✧
This artwork presents different geometric forms such as cubes, cylinders, cones, and spheres.
