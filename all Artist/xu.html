Artist: Xu Bing

Born:1955

Birthplace: Chongqing, China

Profession: Contemporary Artist and Printmaker (Calligraphy)

Died: 29 July 1890

About the Artist

Xu Bing is a Chinese contemporary artist known for his innovative work with language, writing, printmaking and visual communication. He studied at the Central Academy of Fine Arts in Beijing, where he completed his bachelor's degree in 1981 and later earned an MFA in 1987.

Xu Bing's art often explores the relationship between language, communication, culture and meaning. He has created works using invented characters, traditional Chinese printing techniques and other experimental forms.

In 1990, he moved to the United States after receiving an invitation from the University of Wisconsin–Madison. His works have been exhibited internationally at major museums and art institutions. He has also participated in international events such as the Venice Biennale.
