Artist: Mike Winkelmann (beeple).

Born: 20 June 1981

Birthplace: Fond du Lac, Wisconsin, USA.

Profession: Digital Artist, Graphic Designer and Animator.

Died:11 July 2020.

About the Artist

Beeple is the professional name of Mike Winkelmann, an American digital artist, graphic designer and animator. He is known for creating digital artworks, animations, short films, virtual and augmented reality works, and his long-running Everydays project.

His digital art often combines imaginative characters, technology, popular culture and social or political commentary. He also created concert visuals for several well-known musicians and performers.

Beeple became widely recognised for his role in the development of digital art and NFTs. His work Everydays: The First 5000 Days brought significant attention to digital art when it was auctioned by Christie's in 2021.
