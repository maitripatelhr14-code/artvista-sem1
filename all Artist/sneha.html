About the Artist

Sneha Patel is a self-taught tattoo artist whose artistic journey began in her early school years.

Her passion for sketching emerged in the 6th-7th standard. By the 8th standard, she discovered her true calling and aspired to become a professional tattoo artist. With unwavering dedication, she spent the years from 8th to 10th standard refining her sketching skills to perfection.

She mastered a diverse range of subjects including flowers, cartoons, geometric shapes, insects, human skulls, gods and goddesses, and realistic human faces. Her unique strength lies in her ability to analyze live subjects and transform them into detailed sketches.

She stepped into the professional world during her 10th-11th standard. She began practicing extensively on synthetic skin pads using a professional tattoo machine to perfect her technique.

Her professional career on human skin began in the 10th standard, when she created her first-ever real tattoo - a small moon with a cat - on her mother's finger. This marked the beginning of her journey as a tattoo artist.
In the 11th standard, she created her second tattoo, a beautiful butterfly on her sister's hand. In the same year, she tattooed the word "Maa" with intricate designs on her uncle's hand, showcasing her evolving artistry.Continuing her growth, in the 12th standard, she created a custom name tattoo on her other sister's hand.Driven by passion, precision, and a deep love for art, Sneha Patel is transforming personal stories into permanent art.
